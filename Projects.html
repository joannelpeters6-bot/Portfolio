Refund Project

I created a refund tracking system to enhance internal visibility and improve customer service. The system allows for the tracking of refund requests, providing real-time updates on their status and ensuring that customers are kept informed throughout the process. By implementing this system, we were able to enhance our internal process without creating an additional workload because the process is mostly automated.
